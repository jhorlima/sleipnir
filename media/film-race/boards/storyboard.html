Film A: The race

One continuous take on recorder chart paper, 31 s at 30 fps, effects only. Each still is the key moment of its shot. Every number comes from media/shared/data.json.

Act 1: The hook
01: Hook
0.0 to 2.6s

Four pens rest on the paper beside the engine key; "3.97 billion weather rows" is stamped huge, "3.97" in red.

Act 2: The race
02: Load
2.6 to 6.0s

The drum turns and DuckDB's red pen climbs; "742 M rows in 146 s" is stamped, "146 s" in red.

03: Trino builds
6.0 to 11.0s

New sheet. Trino's red step falls from 12,639 s on M to 9,351 s on L; Spark's grey line breaks off on M.

04: Spark fails, then finishes
11.0 to 14.2s

The drum runs into L2; "failed" is stamped on Spark's break and its L2 line reaches 13,803 s.

Act 3: The turn
05: More CPU, slower
14.2 to 19.0s

New sheet. Through Cube, StarRocks and DuckDB get faster on L, but Trino's red pen climbs from 32.4 s to 65.3 s and hesitates.

06: The object store
19.0 to 22.2s

"object store at 2 CPU" is stamped over L2; Trino's red line drops to 24.6 s.

Act 4: Payoff and end
07: Cube Store
22.2 to 26.0s

New sheet. A red line runs flat on the zero line; the Cube logo and "16-34 ms" are stamped on the bass hit.

08: Claim
26.0 to 28.6s

Pens lifted. "Same data. Same limits. Different stories." is stamped in three beats, the last in red.

09: End
28.6 to 31.0s

Pens back down. "Sleipnir" is stamped in red over the repo URL and holds.
